This policy exists so that people can trust that something called "iWantToWatch" is the real project, and so that nobody sells or hosts a copy under this name as if it were official.

## Allowed without asking

- Using the name to refer to the project, for example in articles, videos, tutorials, reviews, and "works with iWantToWatch" integrations.
- Redistributing **unmodified** official source releases or container images under the iWantToWatch name.
- Running iWantToWatch, modified or not, on your own server for yourself, your household, or your friends.
- Saying that a fork is "based on iWantToWatch".

## Needs written permission

- Publishing, distributing, or hosting a **modified** version under the iWantToWatch name or logo.
- Using the name or logo for a commercial product, paid hosting, or a paid service.
- Using the name or logo in app store listings, domain names, company names, or social media accounts in a way that suggests they are official.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; that is what the AGPL is for. If you publish a modified version, give it a different name and logo. Keep the AGPL licence and copyright notices, and make your source code available as the AGPL requires.
